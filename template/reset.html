Factory reset

Plug off of the purifiers other than you would like to register.

Press and hold the "LINK" button until green light starts blinking.
This screen closes when is the operation is complete.

 Nothing appears on the screen?

Please check the below

Is your air purifier plugged in?
It does not matter if your device is on or off for the app to successfully connect.

Is the Bluetooth® ON?
Bluetooth needs to be turned "ON" in your device settings.

Trying to connect multiple devices simultaneously?
Connect only one device at a time.
