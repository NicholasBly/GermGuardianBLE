How to setup voice control

Requirement
To use the voice control, please prepare the below in advance.

Amazon Echo & Alexa Devices
»For more details

Amazon Alexa app
You can download by App Store or Google Play Store. Please login with your Amazon account.

Please check your device settings
Wi-Fi setting is required. You can check the status of Wi-Fi setting in the Device Settings screen.

1, Log in with Guardian TE account
If you have already logged in, skip this step.

2, Account link with Amazon Alexa
Open the "Home" screen of Amazon Alexa app, and say "Alexa, enable Guardian Technologies" to your Amazon Echo device. Then you can proceed setup follow the instruction.
