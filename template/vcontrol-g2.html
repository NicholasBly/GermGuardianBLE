How to setup voice control

Requirement
To use the voice control, please prepare the below in advance. Android device

Google Home
»For more details

App "Google Home"
You can download by App Store or Google Play Store. Please login with your Google account.

Please check your device settings
Wi-Fi setting is required. You can check the status of Wi-Fi setting in the Device Settings screen.

1, Log in with Guardian TE account
If you have already logged in, skip this step.

2, Account link with Google Home
Open the "Discover" screen of Google Home app, and say "OK Google, talk to Guardian Technologies" to your Google Home device. Then you can proceed setup follow the instruction.

2, Account link with Google Assistant
Say "OK Google, talk to Guardian Technologies" to your Android. Then you can proceed setup follow the instruction.
